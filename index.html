Alexandre Covello

Founder

Some companies carry more than revenue: a name people trust, a know-how built over decades, a team that still believes in it. They rarely fail for lack of value. They fail for lack of investment in the tools and the reach the market now demands.

Alexandre spent two decades in venture capital, backing companies at their start. Davray & Co is the other end of that story: taking over the ones that deserve to continue, and giving them what they were missing.

The context

$652B

in undeployed venture capital globally — PitchBook, 2024

Venture capital is at an inflection point. The old playbook no longer works.

Six hundred and fifty billion dollars in undeployed VC capital sits on the sidelines. Fundraising has declined for three consecutive years. LP distributions are at a fourteen-year low. The exit window remains largely closed.

GPs are under pressure from every direction. LPs are demanding liquidity they are not receiving. Founders are navigating boards that were structured for a different market. The relationships and structures that worked in 2021 are under strain in 2025.

The firms that navigate this moment will be the ones that take independent advice seriously — on governance, on liquidity, and on the decisions that cannot be delegated to a lawyer or a placement agent.

This is the moment Davray was built for.
The Firm

A strategic advisory firm built for the venture capital ecosystem.

Davray works with general partners, founding teams, and boards on governance, liquidity, and firm-level decisions.

The firm holds no capital and sponsors no transactions. Its only interest is the quality of its advice. Mandates are taken by introduction.

Advisory
01 GP Advisory

Venture capital firms face decisions at the management company level — on strategy, equity structure, and succession — that sit outside the remit of every other advisor they retain. Davray provides counsel on these questions.

02 Governance

Boards in venture-backed companies are frequently under-structured before they become consequential. Davray works with founding teams, investor representatives, and independent directors on cap table alignment, shareholder dynamics, and board mechanics.

03 Liquidity

Secondary transactions, LP stake transfers, and pre-IPO structuring require an advisor whose interests are fully aligned with the outcome. Davray advises across the full liquidity spectrum in venture capital.

04 Special Situations

Some mandates do not fit a category. When an LP/GP relationship has reached an impasse, or a founder and their lead investor are no longer aligned, Davray takes a small number of these engagements each year, on a retainer basis.
